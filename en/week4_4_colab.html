②Data

Paste as a table or describe the structure

③Graph type

Be specific — treemap · radar · Sankey, etc.

④Design

Color meanings · title · labels · font display

⑤Environment

"Make it run directly in Google Colab"

⑥Verification

"Explain what the code means and what to fix, too"
